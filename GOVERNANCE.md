# FairMarket Governance

## Status

This project is currently stewarded by its founder (Anirudh Allani). The governance described here takes full effect as the contributor base grows. Until then, the founder acts as the decision-maker of last resort, with all decisions made in the open.

## Principles

1. **The rail stays neutral.** Governance exists to protect the protocol's neutrality, not to operate a business on it. No governance decision may favor one storefront, seller, or vertical over another.
2. **Decisions in the open.** All substantive decisions happen in public: RFCs, discussions, and recorded rationale. There are no private roadmaps.
3. **Rough consensus.** We aim for agreement, not unanimity and not simple majority. A proposal moves forward when objections have been heard and addressed, or when remaining objections are recorded and outweighed.
4. **The fee cap is constitutional.** Changing the protocol's fee cap or the one-explicit-fee rule requires the highest level of scrutiny (see RFC process). This is the load-bearing wall of the project.

## Roles

- **Contributors.** Anyone who participates: discussion, issues, RFCs, docs, code.
- **Maintainers.** Contributors with merge rights, appointed by existing maintainers based on sustained, high-quality participation. Maintainers review PRs and shepherd RFCs.
- **Spec stewards.** A small group (initially the founder) responsible for the coherence of the core protocol and the extension system. Stewards approve RFCs that change the spec.

## Decision process

- Small fixes and clarifications: maintainer review and merge.
- New vertical extensions: RFC, community review, steward approval.
- Core protocol changes: RFC, community review, steward approval, spec version bump per [VERSIONING.md](VERSIONING.md).
- Fee policy changes: RFC, extended review period (minimum 30 days), steward approval. Never rushed, never silent.
- Governance changes: RFC, extended review period, approval by stewards. Governance cannot be changed quietly.

## Long-term direction

As the ecosystem matures, stewardship should move toward an independent nonprofit foundation funded by grants and membership dues, so that no single company or individual controls the rail. That transition itself will go through the RFC process when the time comes.

## Code of Conduct

All governance activity is subject to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Enforcement decisions may be appealed to the stewards.
